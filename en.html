    • About Us

    Gen!e is a brand by Macrobiotic Prom, a company making plant-based food for 37 years. Our goal is simple: create meals that are healthier, taste amazing, and respect the planet.

    We use clean, clearly listed ingredients - no hidden additives, no unnecessary processing. We focus on proven raw ingredients, straightforward recipes, and traditional methods that preserve nutritional value.

    From burgers and sausages to tofu, tempeh, and spreads, everything we make is 100% plant-based and produced in Serbia. We meticulously develop each product until it earns its place as a smart, great-tasting choice.

    Gen!e is for anyone who wants more plants on their plate, without compromise - just inspiring food made for everyday eating that's easy to enjoy.
