    • About Us

    Macrobiotic Prom, founded in 1988, is dedicated to producing high-quality plant-based products such as tofu, seitan, tempeh, soy sausages, veggie burgers, among others, using locally sourced, non-GMO ingredients.

    Today, our products are available in health food stores and major supermarkets across Europe.

    Gen!e, our brand designed with the younger generation in mind, makes healthy, plant-based eating more accessible and exciting.

    Here at Gen!e, we believe in nutritious, meat-free products, without missing out on the taste, and enjoying food.

    Macrobiotic Prom is here to help you live healthier, while indulging in the foods you love.

    Here at Microbiotic Prom we are sure you will find products in our range of five categories, that will become a necesity in your kitchen, or workplace.

    Enjoy, and do not forget to take us along wherever the road may take you!
